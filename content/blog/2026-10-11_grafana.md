---
title: "Grafana is now an official app"
date: 2026-10-11
---

[Grafana]({{< relref "docs/usage/installed-apps/grafana.md" >}}) is now available as an official app for Quollix v1.3.7. When the app starts, Quollix automatically collects app and host logs together with resource metrics. Recognized service log formats are parsed into structured fields for filtering and analysis.

Nextcloud users should [enable application logging to the container output]({{< relref "docs/usage/installed-apps/nextcloud.md#logging" >}}) so these entries are included in centralized log collection.
