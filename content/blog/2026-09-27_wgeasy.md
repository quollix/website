---
title: "WG Easy is now an official app"
date: 2026-09-27
---

WG Easy is now available as an [official Quollix app]({{< relref "docs/introduction/apps.md" >}}). It provides a web interface for managing a WireGuard VPN server, so you can access Quollix and its apps remotely without exposing them directly to the internet. See the [WG Easy app documentation]({{< relref "docs/usage/installed-apps/wgeasy.md" >}}) for installation prerequisites and setup instructions.
