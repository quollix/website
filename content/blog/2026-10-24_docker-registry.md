---
title: "DockerRegistry is now an official app"
date: 2026-10-24
---

[DockerRegistry]({{< relref "docs/usage/installed-apps/dockerregistry.md" >}}) is now available as an [official Quollix app]({{< relref "docs/introduction/apps.md" >}}).
It caches Docker Hub images for clients on a LAN, reducing repeated downloads and the chance of hitting pull rate limits.
