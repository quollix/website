---
title: "Quollix 1.4.0: alerts, Grafana dashboards, and custom backup folders"
date: 2026-11-15
---

Quollix 1.4.0 adds:

- **Alerts:** [Email alerts]({{< relref "docs/usage/alerts.md" >}}) notify administrators about high CPU, memory, or disk usage, certificate expiration, and errors in Quollix logs.
- **Grafana dashboards:** The [Grafana app]({{< relref "docs/usage/installed-apps/grafana.md" >}}) includes official dashboards for visualizing metrics and analyzing logs.
- **Custom backup folders:** Multiple Quollix servers can now use the same backup storage by choosing a different [backup folder]({{< relref "docs/usage/settings/backup-server.md#sharing-backup-storage-between-quollix-servers" >}}) for each.
