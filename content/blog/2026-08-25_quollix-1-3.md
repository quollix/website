---
title: "Quollix 1.3"
date: 2026-08-25
---

Quollix 1.3 is now available. This release includes:

- Official app definitions now pin container images by digest, making apps more reproducible.
- App Store and version pages now provide direct install and update actions for available versions. See [app version migration]({{< relref "docs/self-hosting/app-version-migration.md" >}}).
- Service migrations like major version upgrades of Postgres can now be handled by the platform. See [service migration]({{< relref "docs/project/app-store/service-migration.md" >}}).
- Multiple smaller UI improvements.
- Since 1.2, Quollix also gained [dynamic app secrets]({{< relref "2026-08-09_dynamic-app-secrets.md" >}}), and [Vikunja and Grist]({{< relref "2026-08-15_vikunja-and-grist.md" >}}) became official apps.
